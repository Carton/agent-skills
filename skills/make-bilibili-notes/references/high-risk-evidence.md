# High-risk claim and evidence review

Apply this reference to health/medical, legal, investment/financial, tax, safety,
or other advice where an error could materially harm the reader.

## Mandatory process

1. Extract claims from the transcript before searching.
2. Split compound claims into independently checkable statements.
3. Classify each claim: descriptive, causal, diagnostic, treatment, legal rule,
   forecast, return claim, or recommendation.
4. Search current primary or authoritative sources:
   - Health: systematic reviews, clinical guidelines, regulators, major public
     health agencies, and original trials when needed.
   - Legal/tax: statutes, regulations, courts, and official government guidance;
     record jurisdiction and effective date.
   - Investment/finance: regulator filings, audited reports, official market
     data, and primary issuer disclosures; separate historical fact from forecast.
5. Record evidence even when it contradicts the video.
6. State missing population, dose, time horizon, jurisdiction, fees, conflicts,
   alternatives, contraindications, uncertainty, or downside where applicable.

## Evidence levels

- **高**：多项一致的高质量系统综述/权威指南，或适用且明确的
  法律法规、审计文件、监管数据。
- **中**：一项或多项较可靠研究/权威资料，但存在样本、外推、
  时间、地区或执行层面的限制。
- **低**：观察性资料、弱对照研究、二手分析，或存在重大不一致。
- **极低**：机制推断、体外/动物研究、个案、专家观点、营销材料、
  产品展示或利益相关方的未验证陈述。
- **未核实**：没有找到能直接支持该具体主张的可靠来源，或现有信息
  不足以判断。

Evidence level evaluates support for the exact claim, not the prestige of the
speaker or publisher.

## Risk-omission prompts

Check at least:

- Who does the claim apply to, and who was excluded?
- What dose, product, contract, jurisdiction, market, or time window is assumed?
- Are absolute effects, base rates, fees, taxes, adverse effects, and downside
  stated?
- Does correlation get presented as causation?
- Is a surrogate outcome presented as a real-world benefit?
- Is historical performance presented as future expectation?
- Has a rule, guideline, price, or regulation changed since publication?
- Are conflicts of interest, sponsorship, or cherry-picked comparisons omitted?

End with a concise warning appropriate to the topic. Do not replace professional
medical, legal, tax, or investment advice.
